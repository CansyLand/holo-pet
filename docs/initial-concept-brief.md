Initial Concept Brief: Holo-Pet Companion
For Decentraland Festive Trail 2025, commissioned under Creator Connect
Studio collaboration with: Bianca Montesanti

1. Purpose and vision
   We are commissioning a 2x2 voxel virtual pet experience for the Festive Trail 2025 under the theme “Gifts that Keep on Giving.”
   This experience, Holo-Pet Companion, is conceived as an evergreen destination that will stay live and relevant throughout 2026, inviting visitors to return, bond with their chosen creature, and build a sense of ongoing companionship over time. At its center is a radiant holographic egg, a soft glowing voxel object that subtly pulses and releases particles, signalling life and curiosity inside.
   From this egg, visitors can unlock one of four core companions, each with a distinct personality and visual profile:
   A gentle giraffe

An energetic border collie

A sleek black cat

A spirited red dragon

These companions should feel:
Warm, approachable, and emotionally expressive

Clearly readable at a distance in voxel form

Consistent with Decentraland’s aesthetic while standing out as a recognisable “character family”

The overall vision is to create:
A simple, legible core interaction loop so that any visitor can understand what to do within a few seconds

A feeling that this is “my pet in Decentraland,” even if the underlying logic is relatively lightweight

A scene that rewards repeat visits across the Festive Trail dates and beyond, aligning with the idea of a “gift that keeps on giving” through small changes, reactions, and surprises

The collaboration with Bianca should focus especially on:
Character direction and expression of each creature in voxel form

The mood, colour palette, and holographic “egg” language that ties the experience together

Small visual details that communicate care, personality, and charm

2. Timeline and urgency
   Brief sign off: Immediate
   Experience concept and interaction flow locked: As soon as possible after sign off
   Prototype for internal review (basic egg plus one companion working): Target late November, 2025
   Completed build submission (final 2x2 scene and logic): Tues, Dec 9, 2025
   Festive Trail reveal window: Dec 16, 2025 – Jan 6, 2026
   The experience should be stable and ready for visitors from the start of the Festive Trail and remain accessible as an evergreen destination throughout 2026. Any advanced or “nice to have” features should not risk the stability of the core interaction loop for launch.

3. Technical requirements
   Parcel and performance
   Built for a 2x2 scene in Decentraland
   Optimised for low file size and fast loading

Implementation approach
Core logic implemented as straightforward, maintainable scene scripting
Structure the project so that simple tweaks to timings, animations, or text can be made without a full refactor

Core features and interactions
At minimum, the build should include:
A central holographic egg object that:

Visually “breathes” or pulses using light and particle effects
Offers a clear interaction prompt when a visitor is close

Companion reveal and selection:

A simple flow where the visitor can “hatch” or “activate” one of the four pets
Clear visual feedback when a pet is chosen

Companion presence in the scene:

The chosen pet appears in the environment at a readable scale
Idle animations that communicate personality and life

At least one simple interaction per pet, for example:

Petting or greeting animation
A short follow behaviour within a limited radius
A small trick or emote unique to that creature

Light ambient UI or cues:

Minimal on screen text or prompts to guide first time visitors
Optional short tooltip style messages to explain what is happening

Evergreen engagement hooks
Within the project scope, the scene should support at least one or two mechanics that reward repeat visits, for example:
A simple “mood” or “energy” state that changes over time or via interaction
Daily or periodic reactions when the visitor returns, such as a new animation or small visual flourish
A light “care” mechanic that encourages people to interact again, without introducing complex grinding or progression systems

These systems can be lightweight and cosmetic, but they should support the promise that the experience “keeps on giving” rather than being a single one off interaction.
Optional stretch features (if time and scope allow)
Not required for launch, but useful if time permits:
Very simple persistence per wallet (for example, remembering which pet the visitor last chose)
A “photo spot” or small stage where visitors can pose with their pet for screenshots

Audio
A gentle, non intrusive ambient loop that supports a feeling of warmth and care
Optional soft one shot sound cues keyed to key interactions, such as hatching, greeting, or petting

4. Design language and inspirations
   Overall mood
   Warm, playful, and emotionally safe
   A blend of retro virtual pet nostalgia and contemporary voxel charm
   Clearly readable silhouettes and expressions that work at Decentraland camera distances
   Visual world
   Central focus on the holographic egg as a “beacon” that attracts visitors
   A simple, thoughtfully composed 2x2 environment that feels like a small pocket world rather than an empty pad
   Use of soft light, subtle glows, and particles to sell the “holo” concept without overwhelming performance

Bianca’s artistic role
Bianca’s contribution should guide:
The character look and feel across all four pets so they feel like a coherent family

The holographic egg visual language, including surface patterning, glow style, and particle treatments

Any small environmental props or visual motifs that hint at care, play, and companionship

Player journey
The ideal visitor experience:
Sees the glowing egg and is visually pulled toward it.

Understands that an interaction is available and chooses to “activate” it.

Meets their pet for the first time and experiences a short, charming moment of reveal.

Interacts with the pet through at least one simple, satisfying action.

Leaves with a clear sense that they can come back again to see their pet react, play, or “check in” later.

This brief is intended as the starting point for detailed interaction design and asset planning between the studio and Bianca, keeping the core promise of a simple, warm, and enduring virtual companion experience at the center of all decisions.
